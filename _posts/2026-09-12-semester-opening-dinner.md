The autumn semester opened the proper way: the whole group around one table.
<!-- excerpt end -->

A grill in Jeongneung, everyone who has joined the lab this year in one room, and — for one evening at least — remarkably little discussion of convergence criteria.

The group has grown a good deal since the spring, and it is a fine thing to find the table needing more chairs each term. Here is to the semester ahead.
